# Batch 04 — ♥ Hearts, House of Olympus (Greek)

Paste each block one at a time, in the same conversation as your reference sheets.
House accent: vermilion + gold + marble white. Ornament: Greek key/meander, laurel, olive.

## K♥ — Zeus, the Philanderer Emperor

```
Match the established art style of the previous card designs in this conversation.

Ornate playing card K♥ (King of Hearts) for a Chinese trump card game, portrait 2:3 aspect ratio. Half-body character portrait inside a circular ink-wash inset, mirrored "K" and heart-suit indices in opposite corners, thin gold filigree inner frame with Greek key meander and laurel corner details. House accent colors: vermilion, gold, storm white.

Character: Zeus, the magnificent and dangerously charming sky-emperor who rules the House of Desire because no god has loved more recklessly. Crimson-and-gold imperial robes embroidered with storm clouds and thunderbolts, laurel wreath fused into a gold imperial crown, a great black eagle perched on his throne back. He holds a gold thunderbolt loosely like a wine cup, a red silk ribbon (some admirer's favor) tied around it. A single peacock feather is pinned at his collar — Hera is always watching. The grin of a king who has never once been told no.

STYLE: Persona 5 × Hades hybrid illustration — heavy black ink outlines, high-contrast cel shading with painted rim light, dramatic chiaroscuro. Palette: lacquer black, vermilion red, imperial gold, off-white aged-parchment background, plus the accent colors named above. East Asian character design — wuxia and Chinese-opera costuming reinterpreting world mythology. Gold filigree ornamentation, chain and gear accents. Confident theatrical expression, dynamic three-quarter pose, graphic ink-splatter and speedline energy in negative space. Flat graphic background, no photorealism, no western realistic fantasy armor. Clean edges suitable for cutting out as a game asset.
```
