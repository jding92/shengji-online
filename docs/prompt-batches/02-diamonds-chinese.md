# Batch 02 — ♦ Diamonds, House of the Celestial Court (Chinese — home pantheon)

Paste each block one at a time, in the same conversation as your reference sheets.
House accent: crimson + imperial gold + jade. Ornament: auspicious clouds (祥云), cash
coins, dragons.

## K♦ — The Jade Emperor

```
Match the established art style of the previous card designs in this conversation.

Ornate playing card K♦ (King of Diamonds) for a Chinese trump card game, portrait 2:3 aspect ratio. Half-body character portrait inside a circular ink-wash inset, mirrored "K" and diamond-suit indices in opposite corners, thin gold filigree inner frame with auspicious cloud (祥云) and coiling dragon corner details. House accent colors: crimson, imperial gold, jade green.

Character: The Jade Emperor (玉皇大帝), supreme sovereign of Heaven, in maximal imperial splendor. Layered gold dragon robes over deep crimson, flat-topped mianguan crown with twelve strands of hanging jade beads, a gold ruyi scepter across his lap. Seated on a dragon throne wreathed in auspicious clouds, celestial bureaucracy scrolls floating in ranks behind him. Composed, faintly weary majesty — he has signed ten thousand edicts today.

STYLE: Persona 5 × Hades hybrid illustration — heavy black ink outlines, high-contrast cel shading with painted rim light, dramatic chiaroscuro. Palette: lacquer black, vermilion red, imperial gold, off-white aged-parchment background, plus the accent colors named above. East Asian character design — wuxia and Chinese-opera costuming reinterpreting world mythology. Gold filigree ornamentation, chain and gear accents. Confident theatrical expression, dynamic three-quarter pose, graphic ink-splatter and speedline energy in negative space. Flat graphic background, no photorealism, no western realistic fantasy armor. Clean edges suitable for cutting out as a game asset.
```
