## Big Joker — Sun Wukong

```
Match the established art style of the previous card designs in this conversation.

Ornate BIG JOKER playing card for a Chinese trump card game, portrait 2:3 aspect ratio. Full-body character with vertical "JOKER" text indices mirrored in opposite corners, thin gold filigree inner frame.

Character: Sun Wukong, the Great Sage Equal of Heaven (齐天大圣), as the deck's supreme trickster. The Monkey King in full havoc glory: golden chainmail vest over a jester-red opera robe, phoenix-feather cap with two towering feathers in place of a jester's horns, tiger-skin kilt. One foot on a somersault cloud, the gold-banded staff (如意金箍棒) resting across his shoulders with both wrists draped over it, tail curled into a question mark. His grin has beaten death, the dragon kings, and the entire celestial army. Blazing gold flame FX surrounding the card frame — this is the highest card in the game.

STYLE: Persona 5 × Hades hybrid illustration — heavy black ink outlines, high-contrast cel shading with painted rim light, dramatic chiaroscuro. Palette: lacquer black, vermilion red, imperial gold, off-white aged-parchment background. East Asian character design — wuxia and Chinese-opera costuming reinterpreting world mythology. Gold filigree ornamentation, chain and gear accents. Confident theatrical expression, dynamic pose, graphic ink-splatter and speedline energy in negative space. Flat graphic background, no photorealism, no western realistic fantasy armor. Clean edges suitable for cutting out as a game asset.
```
