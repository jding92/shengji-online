## K♣ — Osiris, the Green King Reborn

```
Match the established art style of the previous card designs in this conversation.

Ornate playing card K♣ (King of Clubs) for a Chinese trump card game, portrait 2:3 aspect ratio. Half-body character portrait inside a circular ink-wash inset, mirrored "K" and club-suit indices in opposite corners, thin gold filigree inner frame with hieroglyph cartouche and wheat-sheaf corner details. House accent colors: deep green-black, gold, turquoise.

Character: Osiris, the resurrected king of the afterlife. Skin of pale jade-green, wrapped in black-and-gold mummification silks restyled as layered imperial court robes, tall atef crown with gold plumes. Arms crossed over his chest holding the crook and flail — the flail's three strands ending in small club shapes. Wheat sheaves and turquoise scarabs at the frame base. Faint seams of gold kintsugi lines trace where he was once cut apart and remade. Serene, unkillable authority.

STYLE: Persona 5 × Hades hybrid illustration — heavy black ink outlines, high-contrast cel shading with painted rim light, dramatic chiaroscuro. Palette: lacquer black, vermilion red, imperial gold, off-white aged-parchment background, plus the accent colors named above. East Asian character design — wuxia and Chinese-opera costuming reinterpreting world mythology. Gold filigree ornamentation, chain and gear accents. Confident theatrical expression, dynamic three-quarter pose, graphic ink-splatter and speedline energy in negative space. Flat graphic background, no photorealism, no western realistic fantasy armor. Clean edges suitable for cutting out as a game asset.
```
