# Batch 03 — ♠ Spades, House of the Ravens (Norse)

This is the stress-test batch: it diverges most from the Greek-flavored sheets already
generated, so it proves the unified style lens holds across pantheons. Paste each block
one at a time, in the same conversation as your reference sheets.
House accent: black + gold + ice-blue aurora. Ornament: runes, knotwork, wolf & raven.

## K♠ — Odin, the Wandering Allfather

```
Match the established art style of the previous card designs in this conversation.

Ornate playing card K♠ (King of Spades) for a Chinese trump card game, portrait 2:3 aspect ratio. Half-body character portrait inside a circular ink-wash inset, mirrored "K" and spade-suit indices in opposite corners, thin gold filigree inner frame with Norse rune and knotwork corner details. House accent colors: lacquer black, gold, ice-blue aurora.

Character: Odin, the one-eyed sovereign of the slain, disguised as a road-worn wuxia elder. Wide conical traveling hat (douli) casting his rune-scarred face in shadow, a single ice-blue eye burning beneath it, long grey-black beard braided with gold rings. Black layered robes with knotwork embroidery, the spear Gungnir held like a walking staff — its blade forming a spade silhouette. Two ravens perched on his shoulders whispering into his ears, two wolf silhouettes at the frame's edge. Aurora ribbons and faintly glowing runes drift behind him. He already knows how this game ends.

STYLE: Persona 5 × Hades hybrid illustration — heavy black ink outlines, high-contrast cel shading with painted rim light, dramatic chiaroscuro. Palette: lacquer black, vermilion red, imperial gold, off-white aged-parchment background, plus the accent colors named above. East Asian character design — wuxia and Chinese-opera costuming reinterpreting world mythology. Gold filigree ornamentation, chain and gear accents. Confident theatrical expression, dynamic three-quarter pose, graphic ink-splatter and speedline energy in negative space. Flat graphic background, no photorealism, no western realistic fantasy armor. Clean edges suitable for cutting out as a game asset.
```
